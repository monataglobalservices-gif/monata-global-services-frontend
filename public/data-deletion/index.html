User Data Deletion Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Our Commitment

MONATA Global Services respects the right of individuals to request deletion of their personal information where applicable law permits or requires it.

This policy explains how users of MONATA CRM and related services can request the deletion of personal information, close an account, disconnect an integration, or ask questions about the handling of their data.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion, contact:

Email: monataglobalservices@gmail.com Subject: MONATA Data Deletion Request Website: https://monataglobal.com

In your request, please provide:

  • Your name.
  • The email address associated with your MONATA account.
  • Your organization or workspace name, if applicable.
  • A description of the account, information, or integration you want deleted.
  • Any additional information reasonably needed to identify the relevant account.

Do not email passwords, access tokens, complete payment credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

We will confirm receipt where reasonably possible and may request additional information to verify your identity or authority before processing the request.

3. What You Can Ask Us to Delete

Depending on the circumstances, you may request deletion of:

  • Your MONATA CRM account and associated profile information.
  • Personal contact information.
  • Workspace membership and user-related records.
  • Information held in support requests.
  • Connected-account information stored or processed by MONATA.
  • Email integration data and associated cached information, where applicable.
  • Other personal information that MONATA controls and is legally permitted to delete.

A request relating to one individual's information will not automatically delete an entire organization's workspace or records belonging to other users.

Requests made by an organization's administrator may also be subject to the rights of other individuals and applicable legal obligations.

4. What Happens After a Request

After receiving a request, we will:

  1. Review the request and verify identity or authority where reasonably necessary.
  2. Identify the relevant account and information within the scope of the request.
  3. Determine whether any information must be retained under applicable law, a valid legal obligation, or another lawful basis.
  4. Delete or anonymize eligible information from active systems, or take other appropriate action required by applicable law.
  5. Communicate the outcome or explain any lawful reason why part of the request cannot be fulfilled.

We aim to respond within 30 days of receiving a sufficiently clear and verifiable request. Where applicable law establishes a different deadline, that legal deadline will govern. If an extension is legally permitted and necessary, we will provide notice and an explanation.

5. Retention Exceptions

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where necessary to:

  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Maintain essential audit records or demonstrate compliance.
  • Complete a transaction or resolve a dispute.
  • Respect the rights of other individuals.

Where retention is necessary, we will seek to limit access and use to the applicable purpose and retain the information only as long as reasonably required.

6. Backups and Technical Logs

Information removed from active systems may remain temporarily in protected backups or system logs until those records are overwritten or deleted through the applicable retention process.

Where information remains in backups, it should not be restored to ordinary use except where necessary for legitimate recovery or other lawful purposes. If restored, applicable deletion requests should be reapplied where reasonably practicable.

Actual backup and log-retention periods will depend on MONATA's implemented infrastructure and documented retention schedule.

7. Gmail, WhatsApp, and Other Third-Party Accounts

Disconnecting an integration and deleting information from MONATA are separate actions.

Gmail: You can revoke MONATA's access through your Google Account's third-party access settings or through an available disconnect feature in MONATA CRM. You may also request deletion of eligible Gmail-related information stored by MONATA.

WhatsApp: You may request deletion of eligible WhatsApp-related information held by MONATA. Account-level changes and information retained independently by WhatsApp or Meta are governed by their own processes and policies.

Other integrations: You may need to revoke permissions directly with the relevant provider.

Deleting information held by MONATA does not automatically delete copies held by third-party providers, message recipients, or other independent parties.

8. Account Closure

You may request account closure using the contact details above.

Before closing an account, consider exporting any business information you need to retain, where an export feature is available and you are authorized to access that information.

Account closure may remove your ability to access workspaces, messages, tasks, files, and other records. If you are an organization administrator, consider the impact on other authorized users before requesting workspace deletion.

9. Fees

We do not intend to charge a fee for ordinary data deletion requests unless a fee is expressly permitted by applicable law, such as for a manifestly unfounded or excessive request. Any applicable fee will be explained before processing.

10. Complaints and Further Assistance

If you believe your request has not been handled appropriately, contact us using the email address above and describe your concern.

Where applicable, you may also lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority or exercise other rights available under applicable law.
