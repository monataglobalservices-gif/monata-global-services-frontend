Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Introduction

MONATA Global Services (“MONATA,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects the privacy of individuals and businesses that use our website, services, and MONATA CRM platform.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, use our services, create an account, connect third-party applications, or communicate with us.

MONATA Global Services provides business and digital services, including virtual assistance, website development, social media management, advertising, digital marketing, customer support, and business management software through MONATA CRM.

By using our services, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to read this policy. Where consent is legally required, we will request it separately.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account and contact information

  • Name, business name, email address, telephone number, and account credentials.
  • Organization, workspace, branch, team membership, and user-role information.
  • Information you provide when contacting our support team.

Business and service information

  • Business records, customer inquiries, tasks, documents, appointments, and other information you choose to process through MONATA CRM.
  • Information necessary to provide virtual assistance, website development, marketing, advertising, and customer support services.

Connected account information

  • When you authorize an integration, such as Gmail or WhatsApp, we may process the information permitted by the permissions you grant.
  • For Gmail integrations, this may include email messages, message metadata, recipient information, and information needed to draft, organize, or send messages according to your authorized settings.
  • For WhatsApp integrations, this may include account identifiers, phone numbers, message content, delivery information, and webhook events, depending on the permissions and features enabled.

We do not access a third-party account merely because you have an account with that provider. Access depends on the integration, permissions, and authorization involved.

Technical and usage information

  • IP address, browser type, device information, access logs, timestamps, and security events.
  • Application usage, error reports, and information used to investigate abuse or improve reliability.

Payment and transaction information

  • Billing records, subscription details, payment status, and transaction references where applicable.
  • Payment card or mobile-money information may be handled by the relevant payment provider. We do not claim to store complete payment credentials unless a specific implementation expressly provides for this.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  1. Create and manage accounts, workspaces, and user permissions.
  2. Deliver our contracted services and respond to customer requests.
  3. Operate MONATA CRM and its authorized business workflows.
  4. Connect third-party services at the user's direction.
  5. Draft, schedule, organize, and send communications according to the user's explicit permissions and automation settings.
  6. Process payments, manage subscriptions, and maintain business records.
  7. Protect accounts, prevent fraud, investigate misuse, and enforce our terms.
  8. Provide technical support and troubleshoot problems.
  9. Improve functionality, reliability, and usability.
  10. Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We will not treat a general request for assistance as permission to perform every possible action in a connected account. Actions must remain within the applicable authorization and configuration.

4. Gmail and Other Third-Party Integrations

MONATA CRM may offer integrations with Gmail, WhatsApp, and other external services.

When you connect an account, the relevant provider may ask you to approve specific permissions. You should review those permissions before granting access.

For Gmail, the requested permissions should be limited to those required for the features you choose. Depending on the integration, authorized functions may include viewing messages, preparing drafts, organizing email, and sending messages.

Where approval controls are available, you may require confirmation before specified messages are sent. Automated sending must be limited to rules and permissions you have expressly authorized.

OAuth tokens and other credentials must be protected using appropriate security measures. We will not intentionally expose your connected-account credentials in public-facing application code.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. MONATA does not control their independent processing practices or guarantee their availability.

You may revoke an integration through the relevant provider's security settings or through an available disconnect feature in MONATA CRM. Revoking access may prevent certain features from working.

5. How We Share Information

We may share information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including with:

  • Service providers: Hosting, database, security, communications, analytics, and technical service providers.
  • Connected platforms: Providers such as Google or Meta when required to perform a user-authorized integration.
  • Authorized team members: Users within your organization who have appropriate access permissions.
  • Professional advisers: Lawyers, accountants, and other advisers where necessary.
  • Authorities: Public authorities when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Business successors: A successor or acquiring entity in connection with a legitimate business transfer, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

We do not sell personal information as a business model. We do not authorize service providers to use confidential customer information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing services, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and satisfying legal or contractual obligations.

Retention periods may differ by information type. Backups, security logs, and records required by law may remain for a limited additional period.

Where information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it, anonymize it, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention needs.

7. Data Security

We intend to implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls, authentication, secure communications, protection of secrets, logging, backups, and measures to reduce unauthorized access.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. We therefore cannot promise that unauthorized access, data loss, or other incidents will never occur.

Security statements in this policy describe our commitments and intended practices; they do not constitute a certification or guarantee that every safeguard has already been deployed. We will update our practices and this policy as the platform develops.

8. International Data Transfers

Our service providers or customers may be located in different countries. As a result, information may be processed or stored outside the country in which it was collected.

Where applicable, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that international transfers are handled in accordance with relevant privacy laws and appropriate safeguards.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format.
  • Complain to the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity and protect other people's information.

Deleting an account or information from MONATA does not necessarily delete information held independently by Google, Meta, payment providers, other third parties, or people who have already received your communications.
